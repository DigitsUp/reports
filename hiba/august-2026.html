Overview
The Big Picture

Back-to-school season arrived — and the enrollment pipeline answered.

August was the month enrollment officially kicked off. Three full-list campaigns went out covering values, application access, and school-opening energy — and all three performed. The standout was the Application Portal Now Open email on August 17: 57.4% open rate on 805 recipients, the highest open rate of any full-list send this month. Families who had been considering Hiba were waiting for that message.

List growth pulled back from July's Meta-driven spike — 47 new profiles in August versus 128 in July — but this is expected. July was an outlier driven by a new ad channel ramping; August's pace is a return to steady organic cadence. Net list movement was +27 (47 new, 20 unsubscribes). The channel is established; volume will fluctuate with ad activity.

The welcome series delivered 280 emails across all five touchpoints at a ~39.3% blended unique open rate. Email 1 led at 78.7% — new subscribers are engaging immediately. Email 3 ("Discover How Learning Comes to Life") was the click leader, with 7 clicks from 12 openers — the strongest click ratio in the sequence this month. Email 5 dipped to 10.3% open rate, a notable pull-back from July's 60.8% that reflects the natural softening as subscribers move deeper into the sequence over time.

The LTN flow deepened meaningfully in August. 76 emails delivered across three message groups — as the wave of families who entered the welcome series in July now move further into the nurture sequence. Open rates across all three LTN sends held above 48%, with "Enrollment update for Fall 2026" reaching 61%. The funnel is reaching families in active consideration, not just the top of the list.

Three family spotlights this month each reached Ravenna by end of August — through different starting points, different timelines, different touches. The infrastructure handled all three. The families who are ready move fast.

Overview
The Big Picture

Meta lead ads opened the pipeline — and the welcome series is filling it.

July was the strongest list growth month since January — 153 new profiles, up from 51 in June. The driver is a new acquisition channel: Meta lead form ads, which allow families to sign up directly inside the Facebook/Instagram ad without hitting the website. No landing page friction. No broken popup. July's jump is real growth from a new channel working, not a data artifact.

The welcome series felt it immediately. July delivered 480 emails across the 5-email sequence — nearly 3× June's 173 — as the wave of new July profiles entered the flow. Welcome series performance remained strong at a ~55% blended open rate, and Email 2 ("10 Reasons to Join Hiba Academy") reversed course in a meaningful way: 65.1% open rate and 7.1% click rate, up from 51.3% OR and 0% CTR in June. That's the biggest single-email performance shift in the sequence this year.

On the campaign side, July had two full-list sends. The July Newsletter ("The Heart of a New Beginning") delivered 52.2% open rate on 1,122 recipients — consistent with prior months. The Jul 28 Academic Excellence launch ("Values and excellence come together at Hiba Academy") delivered 41.8% open rate on 1,183 recipients with 18 clicks — a strong debut for a brand-new campaign type.

The LTN flow grew in volume. Three message groups delivered in July across LTN Events, with 10–21 families per send. Track A Email 3 is now reaching families, which means the funnel is deepening — not just filling the top.

Two families entered Ravenna in July. The pipeline is building. With 153 new profiles in July now entering the welcome series and LTN, August conversion volume should grow.

📊 List Growth Context

July's 153 new profiles is the strongest growth month since January — and unlike January, this is organic acquisition at scale. Two factors: (1) Meta lead form ads launched as a new channel, allowing families to sign up directly inside Facebook/Instagram without hitting the website — eliminating the on-site popup friction that has been a known issue. (2) Summer school-season momentum: families begin researching fall enrollment in July. Net list movement: +134 (153 new subscriptions, 19 unsubscribes). The channel is working.

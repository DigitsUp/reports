Klaviyo-driven revenue was $863,092 this summer, down from $984,999 last summer — a 12.4% decline, sharper than our first pass showed once a gap in the 2025 SMS export was corrected (see full report). Email and flows are each down modestly on volume and order-value effects. SMS is the real story: down 53.6% YoY even though audience size and send frequency barely changed. June still ran ahead of last year; the gap is concentrated in July and, especially, August.

SMS campaigns
$47,348−53.6% YoY

The biggest single problem in this audit, and not a reach issue. SMS sent almost the same number of campaigns (26 → 22) to almost the same size list (1.53M → 1.49M recipients, −2.6%). Click rate barely moved (8.26% → 7.17%). But clicks converted to orders 37% less often, placed-order rate fell 45.5%, and revenue per recipient fell 52.3%. Whatever changed happened after the click — offer, timing, or landing experience, not audience or volume.

Email campaigns
$283,545−9.5% YoY

Sent about 30% fewer campaigns (77 → 54) to a broader list — median audience per send is up 75%, from 116.5K to 204.0K. Last summer paired big sends with small, high-converting drops like Liverpool Bucks reminders; most of that targeted layer is gone. Revenue per recipient fell 38.6%. Two blockbuster Labor Day sends alone carried $45.4K last August; nothing this year came close.

Flows
$532,199−6.6% YoY

The most stable channel by far. Flow deliveries are up 61% and orders are up 6.8% — more people are flowing through automations and buying. Revenue per delivery fell 42% though. Cart-recovery flows (Added to Cart + Abandoned Checkout) are the single biggest driver, down $62.5K (−24.3%) on lower order value, not lower engagement. Loyalty & rewards flows are up 54.6%, a genuine bright spot.

Worth flagging
SMS is the largest driver in the whole audit, and it's a conversion problem, not a volume one. Audience size and campaign count are both essentially flat YoY. Revenue is down $54.7K because far fewer of the people who click are actually buying. This is worth a real look at offer strength, send timing, and link/landing experience.
Cart recovery lost value per order, not orders. Added to Cart delivered almost the same volume as last year (−1%) with a slightly better click rate, but AOV inside the flow fell from $194 to $162 (−16%). Abandoned Checkout saw real volume loss too (−24% delivered). Together: −$62.5K.
Email campaigns shifted from targeted drops to broad blasts. About 30% fewer sends, median audience per send up 75%. The Liverpool Bucks loyalty-reminder cadence that drove $17.5K last summer is down to $3.0K (−82.5%) this year on far fewer sends.
Not everything is down. Loyalty & rewards flows (+54.6%), Back in Stock and related flows (+173.6%), and win-back/review flows (+11.3%) all grew. The SMS welcome flow had a slow June during a rebuild but is already back on pace ($5K→$41K, Jun→Aug 2026). Deliverability on email also improved — fewer bounces, spam complaints, and unsubscribes than last summer.

Campaigns — SMS

Klaviyo campaign summary · corrected export

This is the channel the first pass of this audit got wrong. The original 2025 export silently stopped on July 25 and was missing five weeks of August sends. With the complete data, SMS isn't a moderate decliner — it's the single worst-performing channel in the audit, down 53.6%, and audience size isn't the explanation.

Where the drop happens: of the people who click, only 0.31% place an order this year, down from 0.49% last year — a 37% drop in click-to-order conversion, on top of a smaller drop in click rate itself. Engagement got the message opened and tapped nearly as often as last year; something after the tap — offer, landing page, or product mix — is converting worse.

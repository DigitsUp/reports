The biggest week in the series. Attributed email hit CA$76.0K, up 56%, on a stacked calendar: a birthday launch, a three-wave CCB winback, and a Post-Purchase thank-you. The store did CA$112.9K across all channels, up 34%, with email driving about two-thirds of it. The winback doubled campaign volume, so the rates diluted as lapsed subscribers came back, but revenue jumped 80%. And after weeks of decline, list growth finally turned positive.

Campaigns
CA$62,676+80% WoW

Three initiatives stacked: CCB winback (3 waves, CA$24.1K), Post-Purchase Thank You (CA$19.5K, the top-converting send of the week) and the birthday launch (CA$19.1K). Recipients doubled (+101%) on the winback, so open rate and RPR fell even as revenue rose 80%.

Automation
CA$13,311−4% WoW

Roughly flat on more volume. Welcome Series jumped 22% (CA$3,778) on the signup surge, and Abandoned Checkout held. A new Affiliate Flow came online (CA$411). More people entered the flows, but the winback traffic converted at a lower rate, so RPR fell.

List growth
~213K active+3.4% · CA

Positive in all three regions for the first time in weeks: CA +4,230, US +1,700, FR +176 (combined +6,106). The winback re-engaged lapsed subscribers, Canada added 8,080 versus the usual ~700.

Worth flagging
List growth flipped positive. After weeks net-negative, all three regions added members (combined +6,106). The CCB winback pulled lapsed subscribers back into the active window, and sign-ups surged too (Canada's form hit 529 submits, Excellent). This is the clearest upside of the week.
The red rate chips are winback dilution, not a performance drop. Recipients doubled, so open rate (−18.5%) and revenue per recipient (−10.5%) fell by definition when you mail a large lapsed audience. Revenue still rose 80%. Read volume and rates together here.
The Post-Purchase Thank You quietly won the week. CA$19.5K on 238 orders, and the Canada send was the single top send of the whole week (CA$8,921 at a 2.39% click rate). A loyalty touch that converted like a promo.

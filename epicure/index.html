The active audience slid again — CA 128K (−2.8%), US 72.8K (−3.2%), FR 14.3K (−3.1%). About 1,090 came in and 7,600 aged out.

Forms: French converts best this week (7.11%, Excellent), Canada Good (4.15%, 189 submits), and the USA form slipped to Fair (2.30%) — a flip from last week when USA led.

Worth flagging
The retention flows are proving the point. Post-Purchase was up 73% this week and is growing over the month too. With the active list sliding ~3% a week, leaning harder on Post-Purchase and getting Winback live is the clearest way to hold onto people before they lapse.
Browse Abandonment earned CA$0 this week. It dropped off the flow leaderboard entirely — 0 orders, down from CA$274. Worth a quick check on whether it paused or just didn't convert.

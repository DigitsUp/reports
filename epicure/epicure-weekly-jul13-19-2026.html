Softer week, off a peak. Email brought in CA$40.7K attributed (−29% on last week), and the store did CA$83.4K across all channels (−17%). Both came off an unusually strong Back-in-Stock week and landed roughly where things sat two weeks ago (CA$39.3K). Email's share of demand eased to about 49%.

Campaigns
CA$30,454−29% WoW

This week ran Launch, Teaser and Reviews+How-To-Use across CA/US/FR — no restock or giveaway sends like last week. Recipients held roughly flat (−3%); orders and revenue came down off the Back-in-Stock spike. The three Canada sends carried it, led by CA Launch at CA$9.3K.

Automation
CA$10,197−30% WoW

Same three flows do nearly all of it — Added to Cart, Welcome Series and Abandoned Checkout brought in CA$9.5K of the CA$10.2K. Post-Purchase and Winback are still the biggest untapped lever, especially with the active list sliding.

List growth
221K active−3% WoW

The active audience shrank again — CA 131K (−2.8%), US 75.1K (−3.4%), FR 14.7K (−3.1%). Across the three, about 1,050 came in and 8,000 aged out. This is the third straight week the active list has slid ~3%.

Forms: the USA form converts best this week (7.57%, Excellent), Canada is Good (4.55%, 225 submits), and French is lagging (2.68%, Fair) — the one to look at.

Worth flagging
The active list has slipped three weeks running. All three regions are down ~3% again. Bringing Post-Purchase and Winback online is the clearest lever to re-engage people before they age out — the demand's already there, it's just not being caught automatically.
The −29% is off a peak, not a trend break. Last week was the Back-in-Stock restock; this week's Launch/Reviews content landed right in line with two weeks ago. Worth watching, not worth worrying about yet.

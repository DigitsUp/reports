Good week. Email brought in CA$55.1K attributed (+40% on last week), and the store did CA$100.6K across all channels (+47%), with email behind about 55% of it. Campaigns and flows both bounced back from a slow prior week. Here's how it broke down.

Campaigns
CA$41,774+44% WoW

Fewer emails, more sales. Sends went to 696K people — the fewest of the last three weeks — and landed 473 orders, the most. Revenue per email was up 48%. The two Back in Stock CA sends alone did about half the week's campaign revenue.

Automation
CA$13,366+30% WoW

Three flows — Added to Cart, Welcome Series and Abandoned Checkout — brought in CA$12.4K of the CA$13.4K total, and Abandoned Checkout more than doubled off last week. The next lift sits in the automations we haven't leaned on yet — Post-Purchase and Winback are where it comes from.

List growth
228K active−3% WoW

The active list is sliding: all three 30-day segments shrank — CA 135K (−3%), US 77.8K (−3.1%), FR 15.2K (−3.5%). About 9,400 aged out of the active window this week and only ~2,200 came in. Total subscribers rose 0.19% to 386K, but that's the Gleam import — the active audience is the one shrinking.

In and out: organic signups still run through the forms — the EN_CA "free Marry Me Chicken" form led (114 new, ~1,300 submits a month) with Shopify checkout adding 167. Opt-outs are almost all unsubscribes (~900 one-click, ~850 via an email link) — worth testing a "pause" option in the footer so people can take a break instead of leaving for good.

Worth flagging
The giveaway drove volume, not orders. 1,823 net-new subscribers from Gleam, 1 order between them so far. Good for list size, nothing on the board yet — worth a follow-up to convert them.
Post-Purchase and Winback are the next big lever. A manual Back in Stock campaign did CA$12.2K this week, so the demand is clearly there — building out those automations captures it on autopilot, week after week.

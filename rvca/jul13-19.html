Call-out · Attentive desktop sign-up form

Removing the trigger gap doubled desktop sign-up rate

The desktop form ran on a 10-second delay, first session only. Anyone clicking into a collection inside those 10 seconds skipped the trigger and never saw it. It now fires at 5 seconds on every page, so fast movers get it on page two. Live Jul 20 — the read below is the first two days (Jul 20–21 vs Jul 13–14) and sits outside this reporting week.

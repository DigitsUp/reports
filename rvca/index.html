What these four profiles tell us
Flows work when there's a signal.Jarred came back because Browse Abandonment caught his site visit. Without the signal, there's no trigger — and no recovery.
Opens alone don't convert.Matthew has 47 lifetime opens and zero clicks. Gabriella opened for 18 months before each order. Opens are warming, not buying.
The second order is the hardest.All four profiles share it: the gap after the first order is where the lifecycle either compounds or flatlines. Post-Purchase and Win-back exist to close it.
⚠ Data note · attribution and profile selection

Profiles were selected from live Klaviyo data to represent a cross-section of lifecycle stages — two repeat buyers and two one-time purchasers. None of the four orders shown here were click-attributed in Klaviyo, meaning they do not appear in flow or campaign conversion counts. This is expected: Klaviyo's attribution window requires a click within the lookback period, and organic or direct orders fall outside it.

The engagement data (opens, clicks, flow sends) is sourced directly from each profile's event timeline. Order dates and values are from the Placed Order event log. Win-back eligibility is based on the 180-day no-order threshold used in the Win-back Series filter.

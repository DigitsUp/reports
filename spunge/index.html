01.1

The shape of the sale

Three things carried the program, and one dragged it.

Result
Early Access carried the program
$42,481 from 413 orders on the September 2 early-access email alone, which is 51% of all campaign revenue from a single send, at the highest click rate of any email (7.7%).
Result
SMS was the most efficient channel
$12,474 from just 7,974 recipients, $1.56 for every person texted, roughly 12 times email's $0.13. On launch day SMS out-earned email from a list one-tenth the size.
Learning
The cold lead waves underdelivered
The four ComplexCon "Leads Wave" sends reached 149,779 inboxes for $1,477 and 15 orders, at 0.4 to 0.8% click and 1,526 unsubscribes.

Result
The offer landed before the discount did
Early Access at $42,481 was 60% of all email revenue and pulled the highest click rate of the sale. Gating the deal behind the list is what made it work.

The engaged base did the earning
The five EOS sends to the 90-day-engaged list and signup form produced $61,382 from 597 orders, 87% of email revenue, at a 62% open rate.

Cold leads don't convert on a sale blast
The Soka send to the warm base did $7,938, but the four cold Leads waves did $1,477 across 149,779 sends with 1,526 unsubscribes. That volume belongs in a re-engagement flow, not a broadcast.

Result
SMS earns 12x email per recipient
$1.56 against $0.13. On launch day the text produced $8,772 from 4,085 people while the email produced $8,126 from 39,885, roughly the same revenue from a list ten times smaller.
Result
Both texts beat every email on efficiency
$2.15 and $0.95 per recipient were the two highest returns of any send in the sale, at 15.0% and 13.6% click.
Opportunity
The best channel is the smallest list
Only 7,974 subscribers received these. Growing the SMS opt-in is the clearest lever in the account, because the mechanic already converts.
04

Summary

Where the sale landed, and what we are taking into the next one.

What worked
  • Early Access carried it$42,481, more than half the sale, from one email to the list.
  • SMS was the most efficient channel$1.56 per recipient, about 12x email.
  • The engaged base delivered$73,857, 89% of sale revenue, from the warm list.
  • The salehebembury.com cross-promo4,787 clicks into the sale from a separate list, at no send cost here.
Needs attention
  • Cold ComplexCon waves$1,477 from 149,779 sends, with 1,526 unsubscribes.
  • List health4,837 unsubscribes across the window (0.88%), heaviest on Soka and the cold waves.
  • Late-sale fatigueExtra 10% and Last Chance drew high opens but 1.8 to 2.2% clicks and under $4,600 each.
Next steps
  • Grow and lean on SMSSmallest list, best return. Build the opt-in ahead of the next drop.
  • Stop blasting cold leadsMove ComplexCon lists into a re-engagement and sunset flow, not sale sends.
  • Front-load the offerEarly access did the work; the last-chance sends had little left to convert.

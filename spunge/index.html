01.1

The shape of the month

Three things carried the program, and one dragged it.

Result
Overdye early access carried the month
$13,692 from 74 orders on October 15 across the email and the password text, 58% of all campaign revenue, at a $185 average order. The Overdye Pack was the store's top seller at about $22,800.
Result
SMS was the most efficient channel again
$11,560 from 7,387 texts, $1.56 per recipient. The early-access text alone did $5,130 from 4,659 people, 60% of what the email made from 304,781.
Learning
Full-audience sends cost more than they earned
Early access and launch went to the full audience, 602,652 inboxes, for $11,017 and 5,990 unsubscribes. The launch email opened at 28.8% and clicked at 0.37%.

05

Summary

Where the month landed, and what we are taking into Lumina and Black Friday.

What worked
  • Overdye early access$13,692 across email and text, the biggest moment of the month, at full price.
  • SMS$1.56 per recipient across campaigns and flows, against $0.02 for email.
  • Second pair $99$2,599 from 6,847 past buyers; its first email earned the most per recipient of any email.
  • Flows$13,462 on autopilot, rising every full week.
Needs attention
  • Full-audience sends5,990 unsubscribes from the two full-audience Overdye emails, and the launch send clicked at 0.37%.
  • List health12,644 email unsubscribes across the month (1.0% of delivered).
  • Story sends$1,144 from 306,882 inboxes with no offer or access attached.
Next steps
  • Send to the engaged baseKeep full-audience sends for the one moment that needs reach, and throttle them.
  • Text every saleAdd SMS to sale launches and last calls, and keep growing the SMS list.
  • Make second pair permanentBuild the repeat-buyer offer into post-purchase ahead of BFCM.

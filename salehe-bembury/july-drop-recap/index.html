Result
Abandoned Checkout is the highest earning email in the account
Its first email returned $12.60 for every person who received it, from 82 sends at a 94% open rate. No other email in the account earns at that rate.

1,329 emails delivered and 141 clicks recorded, with no orders placed. This flow carries 70% of all flow volume, so it is worth retuning and retesting on the next release before any decision to change the strategy.

One email is live and its 4% click rate is the lowest in the account. The flow produced revenue in the prior 30 days and none in this period.
